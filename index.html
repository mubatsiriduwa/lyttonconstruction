About Lytton Construction

A practical construction partner built around quality delivery.

Lytton Construction (Pvt) Ltd was formed in 2019 to respond to the demand for high-quality construction services in Zimbabwe's growing construction sector. Our work spans roads, water and sewer reticulation, general engineering, maintenance, surveying, civil works, housing, dam construction, machine hire and real estate management.

We combine committed people, dependable equipment and disciplined project execution to deliver solutions that meet client requirements and support long-term infrastructure development.
