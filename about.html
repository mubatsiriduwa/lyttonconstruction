About Lytton Construction

A Zimbabwean construction company delivering civil engineering, road works, utility infrastructure and construction services with a focus on dependable project execution.

Our Story

Growing with Zimbabwe's construction needs.

Lytton Construction (Pvt) Ltd was formed in 2019 in response to the need for high-quality construction in Zimbabwe's fast-growing construction industry.

Our business includes roads, water and sewer reticulation, general engineering, maintenance and surveying, civil engineering, housing construction, dam construction, machine hiring and real estate management. The company was built on commitment, dedication to high operating standards and quality service for clients.

As the business grows in scope and size, we continue to invest in people, equipment and processes that support reliable project execution and long-term development.
